The problem

Three places outreach stalls.

  • 01

    You send connection requests and nothing happens.

    They sit pending, or get accepted and go nowhere.

  • 02

    Your messages read like pitches, so they get left on seen.

    Buyers can spot a template in the first line.

  • 03

    The few replies you get never turn into calls.

    The thread stalls right where it should move to a meeting.

What we'll cover

Seven chapters, from ICP to tracking.

  1. 01

    Niche & ICP

    Define who actually buys: industry, company size, budget and the trigger that makes them need you now.

    ICP
  2. 02

    Signal mapping

    Spot high-intent leads in your industry: hiring posts, funding, new leadership, launches, and people posting about the problem you solve.

    Signals
  3. 03

    The right point of contact

    Find the real decision-maker inside each company, and who to go to when they go quiet.

    Contact
  4. 04

    Offer & messages

    Frame your offer around their problem, not your service. Openers that are specific, relevant and easy to say yes to, with word-for-word 2- and 3-message sequences.

    Messaging
  5. 05

    The psychology of a yes

    What turns a reply into a meeting: curiosity, relevance, proof, a small first ask. And what kills it: pitch dumps, links, early calendar links, vague asks.

    Conversion
  6. 06

    Follow-up sequences

    When and how to follow up before and after the call, so threads don't die and booked calls show up.

    Follow-up
  7. 07

    Infra, testing & tracking

    Profile and Sales Navigator setup, safe daily limits, A/B testing openers, and the five numbers to track: acceptance, reply, positive reply, meetings booked, show rate.

    Tracking

You leave with

Five things you can use on Monday.

  • Your ICP sheet

  • A signal map for your industry

  • Word-for-word message sequences

  • A follow-up cadence

  • A tracking sheet
