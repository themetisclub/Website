From 10% to 60%: why we moved our outreach from cold email to LinkedIn

Shivam Shinde, founder of Reblues Media

For two years, cold email was how Reblues Media found clients. We bought domains, warmed inboxes, wrote sequences and paid for tools that promised the world. Our open rates sat around 10%. Most of our messages never reached a human.

The problem wasn't the copy

We rewrote subject lines, shortened emails and tested every template we could find. Small wins, never a step change. The real issue was the channel: inboxes are guarded by filters, and our buyers (founders and marketing leads at SaaS companies) had learned to ignore anything that looked automated.

Why LinkedIn worked better for us

In 2025 we moved our outreach to LinkedIn. Open rates went to about 60%. Three things made the difference:

  • Context before contact. A buyer can see who you are, what you've made and who you know before they reply.
  • Signals. Posts, job changes, launches and hiring tell you who has a reason to talk right now.
  • Conversation, not campaigns. Short, specific messages read like a person, because they are one.

What we actually changed

We stopped blasting lists. We picked a narrow ICP, watched for signals, engaged before messaging, and wrote one plain message per person that referenced something real. Follow-ups added value instead of "just bumping this". And we tracked every step in a simple sheet, so we knew what to fix.

The catch

LinkedIn isn't magic. Send generic pitches at volume and you'll get ignored there too, or restricted. The channel only works when the system behind it is sound.
