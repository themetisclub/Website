Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The Mētis Club, operated by Shivam Shinde, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

  1. 01

    What we collect

    Your name, email address and WhatsApp number, and optionally your company website, what you sell and a short note about you, when you register for a workshop, join a waitlist, book a consulting session or buy a product. Basic usage data, such as pages visited.

  2. 02

    Payments

    Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your full card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to payment data.

  3. 03

    How we use it

    To deliver your purchase, send workshop details and updates, answer your questions, and improve our content. We'll only send marketing messages you can opt out of at any time.

  4. 04

    Sharing

    We don't sell or rent your data. We share it only with the service providers we need to run the business: payments (Razorpay), form delivery (FormSubmit), hosting (Vercel) and our WhatsApp community, or when the law requires.

  5. 05

    Cookies

    Our site may use cookies and similar tools for analytics and to measure our ads. You can turn cookies off in your browser.

  6. 06

    Data retention

    We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires.

  7. 07

    Your rights

    You can ask to see, correct, or delete your personal data at any time by emailing us.

  8. 08

    Security

    We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

  9. 09

    Changes

    We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page.

  10. 10

    Contact

    Email themetisclub@gmail.com with any privacy question.
