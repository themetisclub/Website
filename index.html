    1. 01

      Niche & ICP

      Define who actually buys: industry, company size, budget and the trigger that makes them need you now.

    2. 02

      Signal mapping

      Spot high-intent leads in your industry: hiring posts, funding, new leadership, launches, and people posting about the problem you solve.

    3. 03

      The right point of contact

      Find the real decision-maker inside each company, and who to go to when they go quiet.

    4. 04

      Offer & messages

      Frame your offer around their problem, not your service. Openers that are specific, relevant and easy to say yes to, with word-for-word 2- and 3-message sequences.

    5. 05

      The psychology of a yes

      What turns a reply into a meeting: curiosity, relevance, proof, a small first ask. And what kills it: pitch dumps, links, early calendar links, vague asks.

    6. 06

      Follow-up sequences

      When and how to follow up before and after the call, so threads don't die and booked calls show up.

    7. 07

      Infra, testing & tracking

      Profile and Sales Navigator setup, safe daily limits, A/B testing openers, and the five numbers to track: acceptance, reply, positive reply, meetings booked, show rate.

    Hosted by Shivam Shinde · Founder, Reblues Media

    I failed at outreach for years. Then it became a system.

    For the last few years I've done outreach on every platform I could find. Prospecting, messaging, framing the offer, and an endless rumble of money burned on email domains and tools that promised everything. Thousands of messages vanished into the void.

    Every self-proclaimed sales coach has the same story: one day an apple fell on their head and they cracked billion-dollar deals. This isn't that.

    I failed. And failed, and failed, and failed again. But after enough failures, patterns started to show. At Reblues Media, cold email was getting 10% open rates. We moved to LinkedIn in 2025 and saw about 60%. Not luck, but a system that books calls every week.

    The Mētis Club is that system, written down, so you can skip the years I lost.

    Is the workshop a sales pitch?

    It's 40 minutes of the actual system. I'll mention the playbook at the end, and that's it.

    Do I need LinkedIn Sales Navigator?

    No. It helps at scale, and we'll show you when it's worth paying for.

    Will this work in my niche?

    If you sell a service to businesses, yes. Chapter one is about defining your exact ICP.

    I don't have case studies yet. Can I still book calls?

    Yes. We cover how to build proof and frame your offer without them.

    Is it recorded?

    Join live for Q&A. Recordings go to the WhatsApp community when available.

    Is it only LinkedIn?

    We start with LinkedIn because it's the sharpest channel for service sellers. The Mētis Club covers the whole sales system: cold email, follow-ups and closing.
